# Ten-user Testnet onboarding

Completion requires at least 10 real people controlling 10 independent wallets. Developer-generated accounts do not qualify.

Recruit community organizers, NGO/diaspora participants, university clubs, Stellar builders, and prospective reviewers. Give each participant the Testnet warning, supported-wallet setup, Friendbot instructions, privacy notice, and this 10–15 minute script:

1. Connect their own wallet on Testnet and confirm the displayed address/network.
2. Read a public project without signing.
3. Complete at least one assigned signed action: create, accept a role, contribute, submit evidence, attest, approve/dispute, arbitrate, or claim a refund.
4. Wait for RPC confirmation and preserve the Explorer link.
5. Answer the short feedback form.

Suggested consent: “I understand this is a public Stellar Testnet exercise using valueless test assets. My transaction and public address will be visible on-chain. I consent to Starpost recording the transaction for verification. Public reports may abbreviate my address unless I separately consent to display it. I will not upload personal, KYC, confidential, or third-party data.”

Support must never ask for a secret key or recovery phrase. A moderator may help with network selection, Friendbot, simulation messages, and retries. Remove names/emails from the verification export; use participant aliases.

Record each interaction in `evidence/user-interactions.csv`. Completion means 10 unique consented participants, 10 unique addresses, 10 successful unique transactions against an approved Starpost contract, an expected event or actor match, and reproducible verification. Do not count failed, duplicate, team-controlled, or unrelated transactions.

Feedback questions:

- Ease of wallet connection (1–5)
- Clarity of the project flow (1–5)
- Understanding of milestone verification (1–5)
- Trust in the payout rules (1–5)
- Most confusing step
- Most valuable feature
- Would you use it again?
- One improvement request
- Consent to use an anonymized response

Only populate `USER_FEEDBACK_SUMMARY.md` from authentic collected responses.
