# Level 5 privacy and evidence policy

## Data separation

The private response source contains name, email, wallet, transaction, ratings, comments, and consent. It must not be committed, attached to a public issue, placed in analytics, or included in screenshots.

The public evidence contains a generated participant ID, consented wallet and transaction proof, action, ratings, redacted comments, and verification status. Names and emails are removed. Public transaction proof still allows an observer to inspect the on-chain source account, so participants must receive that warning before acting.

## Retention and access

- Restrict the raw Form, Sheet, and Excel export to the project owner and explicitly authorized judges.
- Retain the raw cohort only through the submission/review window, then delete or re-consent according to the program’s evidence policy.
- Do not collect secret keys, recovery phrases, identity documents, precise location, financial information, or evidence files through the feedback form.
- Honor a participant withdrawal request by removing their off-chain response and excluding the record from future cohort reports; immutable Testnet history cannot be deleted.

## Verification boundary

The verifier checks local schema, uniqueness, consent, approved contract, RPC success, Horizon success, expected event, and actor/source matching. It does not prove that a person supplied a legal identity. The operator remains responsible for recruiting real independent people and excluding team-controlled or automated wallets.

The public workbook is a prepared template until authentic responses exist. Empty templates, sample records, and pending status files are never described as completed user evidence.
